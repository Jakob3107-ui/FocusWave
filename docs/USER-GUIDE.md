# FocusWave — quick guide

Welcome to your new computer 💜 Here's everything you need. **Meta** is the Windows key (⊞).

## The desktop

- **Top bar** (at the top): the menus of the app you're using, the **Focus** button, Wi‑Fi/sound/battery, and the clock. Click the clock for a calendar.
- **Dock** (at the bottom): the **FocusWave button** opens the Start menu. You can also just press **Meta** and start typing to find any app, file or setting. Next to it are your apps. Right‑click an app → *Pin to Task Manager* to keep it in the dock.
- **Notifications** and quick toggles (Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, Night Light, Do Not Disturb) are in the icons on the top right.

## Focus Mode ✨

When you need to concentrate, Focus Mode blurs everything except the window you're using.

- **Turn on:** press **Meta+F**, click the **Focus** button in the top bar, or right‑click a window's title bar → *Focus Mode* (it may sit under *More Actions*).
- **Turn off:** do the same again, or **double‑click the blurred area**.
- Switching to another app (from the dock or with Alt+Tab) moves the focus to that app.
- Settings: **Customize → Focus Mode**. Change how blurry it gets, darken the background, or keep the dock visible.

## Make it yours — the Customize app

Open **Customize** from the dock (the colorful sliders icon). Every change shows up right away.

| Page | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Look** | Light, Dark or Automatic (light by day, dark at night), favorite color, wallpaper (or your own photo) |
| **Layout** | *Mix*, *Mac‑like* or *Windows‑like*; dock size, position (bottom/left/right), floating, auto‑hide, transparency → **Apply to my desktop** |
| **Windows & Effects** | traffic‑light or Windows buttons, opening/minimizing animations, animation speed, wobbly windows 🍮, glass blur |
| **Focus Mode** | blur, darkening, fade, keep dock visible, shortcut |
| **Fonts** | font and size for everything |
| **Themes** | **save** your current look with a name, switch between saved looks in one click, and **export/import** `.fwtheme` files to share |
| **Advanced** | edit panels & widgets, every KDE setting, reset to FocusWave defaults |

Tip: right‑click the desktop → *Enter Edit Mode* to add widgets (weather, notes, photos…) or move things around.

|---|---|

| **Alt+Tab** | switch between windows |
| **Meta+W** | see all windows (Overview) |
| **Meta+D** | peek at the desktop |
| **Meta+←/→/↑** | snap a window left / right / maximize |

| **Meta+.** | emoji picker 😊 |

| **Meta+L** | lock the screen |

## Apps

- **Zen** (first icon in the dock) is your web browser. Your tabs live in the sidebar on the left. *Compact mode* hides the toolbars for a clean full‑page view, and *split view* shows two sites side by side; both are in the right‑click menus and Zen's settings. Firefox add‑ons work in Zen.
- **Discover** (shopping bag icon) is the app store. Search for Spotify, Discord, WhatsApp (*WasIstLos*), Zoom, LibreOffice, GIMP, Steam and lots more. Updates show up here too.
- Microsoft 365 and Google Docs work in Zen.
- **KDE Connect** links your phone: notifications, sharing files and texting from the laptop. Install *KDE Connect* on Android, or *KDE Connect* from the App Store on iPhone.

## Battery

Click the battery icon in the top bar to switch between **Power Save**, **Balanced** and **Performance**.

## If something looks wrong

Customize → Advanced → **Re‑apply everything**, or **Reset to FocusWave defaults**. Saved themes are kept.
